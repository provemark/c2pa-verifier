# Step 144 — ADR-0005: when this verifier may be stricter than `c2patool`

*2026-09-25. A decision. No code or test behaviour changed.*

## Why a revision so soon

Step 143 recorded Bing's `self#jumbf=c2pa/…` URIs as a difference *by
design*, on the strength of C2PA 2.4 §8.4.2.1's normative text. Maurice
then asked for an honest opinion: should this verifier follow `c2patool`
here after all? The honest answer was yes, and it was a different
answer from the one that led to step 143.

The existing differences by design each protect something. A timestamp
authority is trusted only through an anchor, because otherwise trust
rests on observation. A CAWG identity is refused until it is validated,
because otherwise the verdict covers a credential nobody examined. The
Bing URI protects nothing. Its meaning is unambiguous, its label must
still be the claim's own manifest, and everything it leads to is checked
afterwards. Refusing it only produces an `Invalid` that the reference
implementation, and the authors of the spec's examples, would not give.
A verifier that says "invalid" without cause teaches its users to ignore
the word.

## What changed

- **`docs/adr/ADR-0005-when-to-be-stricter-than-c2patool.md`**: this
  verifier is stricter than `c2patool` only where the strictness prevents
  a wrong `Valid` or trust in something unchecked. Elsewhere it follows
  `c2pa-rs`, even where that is more lenient than the normative text. A
  *by design* row must name what it prevents; otherwise it is a gap.
- **`docs/comparison.md`**: the introduction points to ADR-0005 for what a
  *"named reason"* must be. The Bing URI row moved from *"differs by
  design"* to *"`c2patool` can do more"*, as deferred until box hashes,
  with SPEC-042 as the first step.
- **SPEC-041 amendment 3** (weight C, confirmed by Maurice van Loon):
  amendment 2's *"SPEC-042 will not be written"* becomes *deferred*.
  The comment in `FirstPieceSequenceTest.php` says the same.

## Open

The other rows under *"Where this verifier differs by design"* (38
rows) have not been checked against ADR-0005. Each needs its protection
named, or it moves to the gaps. That is a review of its own, not done
here.
